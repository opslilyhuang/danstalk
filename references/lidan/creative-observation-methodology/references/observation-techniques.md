## Advanced Observation Training

### Film Analysis Techniques

**Camera Scheduling Analysis:**
- Track shot composition and movement
- Identify director's intent behind framing choices
- Note how visual rhythm supports narrative pacing

**Rhythm Deconstruction:**
- Map out pacing changes throughout the piece
- Identify tension and release points
- Analyze how rhythm affects audience engagement

### Life Observation Practice

**Daily Scenarios to Analyze:**
- Social interactions in different contexts
- Emotional reactions to common situations
- Subtle behaviors that reveal character
