# Performance Techniques

## Core Performance Mindset: Restoring vs Acting

**When to use:** When performing memorized material on stage.

### The Principle
Performance is about **restoring** (recreating the scenario), not **acting** (demonstrating a trait).

### How to Execute
1. **Shift your focus:** Do not focus on the words in the document. Focus on the scenario/context imagined during writing.
2. **Adopt the mindset:** "I am already on stage, starting to tell the story."
3. **Be the character:**
   - Avoid: "I need to act righteous now" (Acting a trait)
   - Adopt: "I am a cop arresting a suspect" (Being the character in the situation)
4. **Achieve naturalness:** By concentrating on the scenario, not the performance.

### Troubleshooting
- If you frequently forget a joke: Stop telling it. It means you don't like it.

## Handling Performance Interruptions

**When to use:** When your performance is interrupted by an unexpected event and you're thrown out of your prepared context.

### Assess Your Skill Level
- **Beginner/Unskilled at improvisation:** Use the Simple Method
- **Experienced/Skilled at improvisation:** Use the Advanced Method

### Simple Method (for Beginners)
1. Say: "哎呀，我被打断了" (Oh, I got interrupted)
2. Deliver this line with a wronged/upset tone (委屈地这么说)
3. The audience WILL laugh at this
4. After the laugh, continue with your prepared material
5. **Key principle:** Sincerity itself is charming

### Advanced Method (for Experienced Performers)
1. Identify who interrupted you
2. Engage with them directly
3. Requires confidence and improvisational skill

### Core Principle
You must handle the interruption - ignoring it is not an option. The simple phrase works because it's honest and relatable.

## Emotional Connection Technique

**When to use:** When starting a comedy performance or segment.

### Why Emotion Must Come First
- Otherwise the audience won't listen
- Human brains naturally want to hear stories
- Stories must contain emotion

### How to Summon Emotion
1. **Tell stories, restore/recreate that story**
2. **You're restoring that emotion, calling it up**
3. Even when not feeling it: "Even if you're unhappy today, you still have to perform"

### The Purpose of Verbatim Scripts
The verbatim script's purpose is to call up emotions. Scripts serve as emotional anchors, not just text to memorize.

### Controlled Spontaneity
- **For beginners:** Rely on prepared emotion, not spontaneity
- **For experienced performers:** Control the boundary, but inside the boundary it's chaotic/free
- Preparation is control: writing scripts and rehearsing creates the boundary

### Practical Application
During preparation, imagine the venue (100, 200, 2000 people) and the corresponding scene. Unexpected joy comes when the actual response exceeds your imagination.
