# Verbatim Script Writing Method

## Write the Verbatim Script

**Core process:**
- Write down exactly what will be said on stage, word for word
- While writing, imagine the future stage performance
- Capture the specific situation and rhythm of each line

**Purpose:**
- Removes filler words ('then', 'uh', 'ah') through pre-performance polishing
- Acts as a pre-performance rehearsal
- Serves as a "directory" to recall creative state and future performance images
- Do not summarize - the text is an index, performance relies on visualizing imagined scenes

## Apply Formatting for Rhythm

**Match format to delivery style:**
- **Hulan Style**: No punctuation, no paragraph breaks, dense text (matches fast, dense delivery)
- **Rock Style**: Short lines (6-7 words), looks like poetry (matches short, punchy delivery)
- **Yan Yi Yan Yue Style**: Clean, collaborative editing in shared docs

## Editing Principles

**Delete aggressively:**
- Delete words until no more can be deleted
- Remove narrative bridges (e.g., "My dad asked me...")
- Directly enter the role/performance instead of telling the story
- Goal: Restore the situation, not tell the story

**Stage optimization:**
- Use body language/acting to replace words
- "Use acting if possible, don't just tell"
- Don't be a cold observer; enter the character

## Content Constraints

**Avoid these elements:**
- NO Puns (谐音梗): Wordplay accessible to amateurs
- NO Internet Slang: Memes or casual web language
- NO Plagiarism: Harms long-term creative capability

**Principle:** "Take from the high to get the middle" (取法其上得其中) - Learn from the best, not mediocre sources

## Feedback Loop

**Get expert review:**
- Find a mentor/expert to edit the script
- **CRITICAL**: Keep document "track changes" (修改痕迹) to observe decision-making process
- Learn their logic from the edits, not just the final result

**Handle disagreement:**
- If you don't agree with feedback, you don't have to accept it
- Listen to it to avoid self-doubt
