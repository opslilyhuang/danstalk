# Employment Transaction Analysis

## When to Use This Skill
- Evaluating whether to leave current employment
- Assessing if workplace frustrations are solvable or industry-inherent
- Deciding between employment, freelancing, or starting a company
- Understanding the economic value of your current role

## Core Concepts

### The Employment Transaction
Work is an economic exchange where you trade time and talent through a company to obtain money from the market.

### Coase Theorem Application
Companies exist to reduce transaction costs—the time and effort required to close deals directly with the market.

### Transaction Costs
Resources expended on:
- Explaining concepts/services to clients
- Legal fees and compliance
- Overcoming market resistance
- Building credibility from scratch

## Analysis Procedure

### Step 1: Identify the Source of Frustration
Determine if difficulty stems from:
- **Company Stupidity**: Internal inefficiency, poor management, unnecessary bureaucracy
- **Market Cruelty**: Unreasonable client demands, audience preferences, platform constraints inherent to the industry

> **Note**: Market cruelty exists regardless of which company you work for. It cannot be escaped by job switching within the same industry.

### Step 2: Quantify Transaction Costs
Estimate the effort required to:
- **Trade with current company**: Internal politics, processes, compensation negotiations
- **Trade directly with market**: Client acquisition, self-marketing, legal compliance, business operations

### Step 3: Apply Decision Rule

```
IF (Effort trading with the company > Effort trading directly with the market)
THEN (Leave the company)
ELSE (Stay or negotiate better terms)
```

### Step 4: Consider Strategic Timing

**Early Career Strategy**:
- Stay initially to gain industry experience and help lower industry transaction costs
- Build credibility and learn market dynamics through established channels

**Experienced Professional Strategy**:
- Once industry costs are lowered and you have accumulated experience
- Leaving to start a new company becomes rational with higher success probability

## Decision Outcomes

Based on the analysis, determine:
1. **Stay**: If company reduces transaction costs significantly vs. going independent
2. **Leave**: If independent market trading is more efficient than current company
3. **Pivot**: If market cruelty is the primary issue, consider changing industries entirely

## Constraints

This framework does not apply when:
- The "market" itself is the source of the cruelty (unreasonable clients, platform biases)
- Non-economic factors are primary drivers (personal values, life circumstances)
- Industry has fundamentally shifted making previous transaction cost estimates invalid
