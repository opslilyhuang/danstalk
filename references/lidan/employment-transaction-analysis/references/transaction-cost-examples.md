# Transaction Cost Examples by Industry

## Tech Industry
**Company-reduced costs**:
- Sales team handles client acquisition
- Legal department handles compliance
- Marketing team builds brand recognition
- HR manages hiring

**Market trading costs**:
- Building personal brand from scratch
- Finding and negotiating with clients individually
- Handling own legal and tax compliance
- Unstable income and cash flow management

## Creative Industries (e.g., Stand-up Comedy)
**Company role**: Bookings, venue management, promotion
**Market costs**: Explaining your act to every bar owner, self-promotion, negotiating each gig

## Consulting
**Company role**: Client relationships, proposals, project management
**Market costs**: Business development, brand building, delivering all services solo

## Distinguishing Market Cruelty vs Company Stupidity

### Market Cruelty (Industry-Inherent)
- Picky clients across all companies
- Platform algorithm changes affecting all creators
- Industry-standard payment terms (e.g., net-60)
- Audience preference shifts
- Regulatory changes affecting entire sector

### Company Stupidity (Internal-Specific)
- Unnecessary approval layers
- Inefficient internal tools
- Poor communication between teams
- Arbitrary policies that competitors don't have
- Management decisions that contradict market reality

### Example Scenarios

**Scenario 1**: Software developer frustrated by slow code reviews
- If competitor companies have faster processes → Company Stupidity
- If all companies in your industry have heavy compliance → Could be Market Cruelty

**Scenario 2**: Content creator dealing with demonetization
- If platform-wide issue affecting all creators → Market Cruelty (leaving company won't help)
- If specific MCN mishandling claims → Company Stupidity

**Scenario 3**: Sales person facing impossible quotas
- If quotas align with realistic market opportunity → Company issue (or poor market fit)
- If entire industry is shrinking → Market Cruelty (leaving won't fix problem)
