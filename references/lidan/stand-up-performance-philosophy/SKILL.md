# Stand-up Performance Philosophy

## Core Principle

**Performance purpose:** Make people believe
- Start by believing you
- Then slowly understand you
- Finally love you

**Sincerity is the first priority** (stated twice in source)
- Stand-up comedy IS performing as yourself (脱口秀就是在演自己)
- Through performance, share your REAL values
- On stage, you cannot hide what kind of person you are
- This is a logical inevitability

## Self-Exposure Principle

**On stage, share your entire self:**
- Your "performed" happiness, pain, life choices, anger, pride
- All contain and shape your values

**Why stand-up can be dangerous:**
- Sometimes exposes too much
- Can lead to feeling hurt by post-performance evaluations

**Reference insights:**
- Wang Shuo: "All exposed, my secret experiences and hidden feelings"
- Writer friend: "Writing novels, in the end, is competing with personality"
- Stand-up comedians also compete with personality
- What ultimately attracts audiences is the personality you cannot hide

## Why Movie Stars Are Attractive

- Even playing characters, not themselves
- Camera close-ups capture their real cries, real laughs
- Those emotions ARE real - your emotions follow theirs
- Someone who can expose emotions real enough to make you believe becomes a star

## Self-Protection Methods

**Actors protect themselves by:**
- Remembering everything is fake
- Entering a character

**For stand-up comedians:**
- Ensure emotions are ALWAYS real
- Text can be fabricated, real life can be modified
- In daily life, live authentically with nothing to hide
- Then on stage, you won't fear exposure
- The stage is a safe place - bare your heart, receive tolerance
- Content that can't be said in life (e.g., racial discrimination) doesn't matter on stage

## Audience Relationship

**Must be HORIZONTAL (equal), not hierarchical:**
- You are sharing values
- Gathering those psychological facets that resonate with you
- This is a moving relationship, not one that can be begged for
- When you discover someone is trying to please you, you cannot laugh

## Purpose of Performance

**WRONG PURPOSE:**
- To please that day's audience
- To get laughs at any cost

**RIGHT PURPOSE:**
- To become a stronger stand-up comedian through making audiences laugh

**Long-term perspective:**
- This is a lifelong career, inseparable from everything you are
- "You are your purpose" (你是你的目的)
- Don't look for audience - let audience find you
- Train yourself hard; the stronger you are, the more audience will find you
- Explosive reactions, fame, fortune are byproducts
- Pursuing these leads to nothing, or becoming a mediocre or bad person

## Daily Self-Assessment

**Before sleep, examine only one thing:**
- Have you continuously improved?
- Did you gain something today that you didn't have yesterday?

**Charlie Munger's advice:**
- Many excellent people already did this before reading the advice
- Life's purpose is to become a better self
