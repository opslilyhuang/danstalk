# Manual Origin and Contextual Framework

## Identify the Origin of the Text
The manual was originally authored specifically for the author's colleagues (internal staff). The direct address in the text targets this internal group.

## Interpret the Tone and Style
Because the original audience was colleagues, the writing style may appear overly familiar or casual.

- The author notes the reader might feel the author is "猛跟我套近乎" (trying very hard to act familiar/close)
- Readers should acknowledge and excuse this familiarity as a byproduct of the text's origin

## Understand the Publication Intent
The author's goal in publishing is to attract more people to join the industry.

- The relationship between the author and the reader is flexible
- The reader can be a colleague OR a competitor ("可以做竞品")
- Both competitors and colleagues are welcomed by the author
