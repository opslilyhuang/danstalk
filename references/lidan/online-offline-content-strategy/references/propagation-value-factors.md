## What Makes Content Shareable Online

### High Propagation Value
- Relatable themes (everyone experiences this)
- Clear psychological insight
- Universal human truths
- Strong emotional resonance

### Low Propagation Value
- Insider jokes (only funny to those in the room)
- Isolated absurd incidents
- Context-dependent humor
- Local references without broader relevance

### Strategy
- Build live set with mix: anecdotes for laughs + themes for recording
- Record sets specifically with online audience in mind
- Edit for clarity - online audience can't ask questions
