# Online vs Offline Content Strategy

## Offline (Live Theater) Strategy

**What works:**
- Telling stories about other comedians (insider jokes)
- Using "isolated funny incidents" (anecdotes) that deviate from normal life
- Audience already invested in the live experience

**Limitation:**
- These anecdotes are funny live but have poor propagation value online

**Warning:**
- Do not compete with traditional forms (e.g., Crosstalk) in absurd anecdotes
- They are experts at this

## Online (Broadcast) Strategy

**Required elements:**
- Must have Thematicity
- Must relate to everyone's life and psychology (universal resonance)

**Method:**
- Focus on "true mental activities" rather than just surface events

**Principle:**
- "If it happens to you, it happens to many"
- Human brains share limited thinking patterns

**Goal:**
- Explain the shared reality clearly, thoroughly
- Make it funny

**Comparison example:**
- **Absurd approach**: Sprite + Chili Oil (isolated, weird incident)
- **Value approach**: Drinking Sprite = Giving up on life (psychological truth)

## Strategic Selection

**When choosing material for a show:**
1. Identify the platform/context (live theater vs online broadcast)
2. Match material to audience expectations
3. Live: prioritize anecdotes and insider humor for immediate laughs
4. Online: prioritize themes and universal psychological truths for shareability
