## Northeast Dialect Insight

**Why Northeast dialect is funny:**
- Humor partly comes from lively word usage
- Pronounce ordinary words very vividly

**Key distinction from other dialects:**
- Other dialects: humor comes from strangeness, a 'spectacle' type humor
- Northeast dialect: doesn't rely on strangeness (very close to standard Mandarin)

**Different reaction mechanisms:**
- Other dialects: 'Why does this person talk like this?'
- Northeast dialect: 'Words can be said like this?'

**Learning opportunity:**
- Every moment you feel 'Words can be said like this?' is a moment to learn articulation technique
