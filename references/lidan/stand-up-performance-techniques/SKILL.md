# Stand-up Performance Techniques

## Technique A: Articulation (咬文嚼字)

**Sound symbolism principle:**
- Each word's pronunciation relates to its meaning
- Examples: '大' (big) - mouth shape enlarges; '小' (small) - mouth shape shrinks
- Words like 轻、重、缓、急 should be pronounced accordingly

**Practical application:**
- Pre-chew every word you'll say on stage
- Each word can have its own flavor - if it doesn't, make it have one
- Consider word position and intent:
  - Do you want audience to hear clearly?
  - Mislead the audience?
  - Deliver a punchline?
  - Make audience feel pain or joy?
- Each intent has only ONE best pronunciation

**Exercises:**
- Read '感情' (emotion) with emotion, '理性' (rationality) rationally
- Read '我可真高兴' (I'm so happy) with different emotions

**Cautions:**
- NOT recommended for beginners to take diction classes
- Stand-up is performing yourself - voice shouldn't differ from real you
- Voice becoming too 'broadcasting' or 'stage-like' affects sincerity
- Focus on modifying lines, not pronunciation technique

## Technique B: Vivid Restoration (津津有味)

**Core concept:**
- Making something vivid is a basic quality of stand-up
- Method: Restore the situation you're describing before your eyes
- Let listeners feel as if they're seeing it

**Deep imagination:**
- While speaking, think of the situation in your mind
- Text is just index, catalog - situation is what truly needs to be shown

**Sources of situation:**
- Memory: "My dad said at the time..."
- Observation: "I saw such a guy on the subway..."
- Pure fabrication: "Martians greet each other like this..."
- Truth or falsehood doesn't matter - on stage, it becomes real

**Restoration vs. Performance:**
- Restore how something looks in YOUR mind
- Phone call example: Don't just make gestures; restore how you actually call
- Key is RESTORATION, not performance

**Values over technique:**
- Someone who can restore, even with technique deficits, will be decent
- Someone who cannot restore, even with great technique, won't seem like stand-up
- If performance overshadows values, even discard the performance

## Technique C: Attention and Focus

**Fundamental principle:**
- Attention should first be placed on the situation you're restoring
- When performing two-person dialogue, don't think about where to look
- If attention is concentrated, your eyes naturally look at the person in your situation

**Practical guidance:**
- When concentrated enough, you won't think about where to look
- Situation will guide your gaze to every corner of your stage world
- Audience and camera are naturally in these corners

**Example scenarios:**
- Asking "Did anyone come by subway?" - attention naturally goes to audience
- Self-deprecating "Wow, young people can all afford cars" - attention returns to self
- Mocking the world - eyes naturally go to camera

**Simple practical technique:**
- Extend right arm, make a fist
- Place at focal point between eyes
- Imagine this fist is 3 meters away
- Stare at that spot when unsure where to look
- Result: Attention appears concentrated whether live or on camera

## Technique D: Emotional Authenticity

**Emotion as connection:**
- Emotion is the 'wormhole' connecting you with audience
- To influence a brain, enter through emotion, then persuade reason

**Writing/performance principle:**
- If you can act it, don't just say it
- If you can move, don't stand still
- If you can use a story, don't reason
- Even George Carlin used imagery/emotion before preaching

**Emotional indicators:**
- "Emotion went up" = resonated
- "Everything was fine but no energy" = no emotional investment or resonance

**Professional approach:**
- Creation stage: Use reason to arrange performance
- Check: Good story opening? Vivid example? Lively eyes?
- On stage: Everything goes to emotion, even your own emotion goes to imagined emotion

**Controlled loss of control:**
- Calligraphy principle: Brush movement between familiar and unfamiliar
- Too controlled = stiff; completely out of control = chaos
- On stage, occasionally break through script's imagination
- You'll be infected by yourself - brain rewards with dopamine

**Performing weaknesses:**
- "Acting a role is acting weaknesses - find weaknesses"
- Bring that emotion to stage
- Not telling all secret experiences, but restore that hidden feeling

**Protection principle:**
- Only emotion is real
- This conversely protects you
- Stories can be modified - key is facing your emotion
- That emotion you might not want to expose could be the stage highlight
