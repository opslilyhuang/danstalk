# Creative Production Workflow

## When to Use This Skill

Use this skill when:
- Conducting internal script reading meetings
- Preparing for or conducting pre-interviews with guests
- Pitching creative concepts or options to collaborators
- Managing creative team dynamics and decision-making

## Internal Script Reading Meetings

### Preparation Requirements
- Never enter empty-handed
- Bring a complete first draft or at minimum written ideas
- Principle: "You can write badly, but just write"

### Meeting Conduct
- Focus on solving problems and moving forward
- Minimize gossip and irrelevant jokes
- Maintain goal clarity throughout

### Decision-Making Framework
- **Core Principle**: "Creation is Dictatorship"
- Brainstorming is encouraged, but the responsible person makes final decisions
- Responsibility hierarchy:
  - Script Editor decides for guest-related content
  - Actor decides for their own performance

### Feedback Protocol
- Use "Yes, and..." technique from improvisation
- Negativity must be accompanied by a better creative idea
- Never offer pure rejection without alternative

## External Pre-Interviews & Pitching

### Preparation
- Watch extensive interviews of the guest
- Imitate their tone and rhythm
- Treat the guest as a character you are writing

### The Pitch Technique
- Do NOT ask Yes/No questions ("Will you buy this?")
- Give options instead (High/Medium/Low)
- Force choice of "which" not "if"

### Questioning Technique

**DON'T ask "Why"**
- Most people don't know their true motivations
- Creates defensiveness

**DO ask "What"**
- Ask for facts, behaviors, and details
- Example: Instead of "Why did you return?", ask "What was the first thing you did when you returned?"

### On-Site Execution
- Complete at least 50% of the script on-site
- **Jie-cha**: Interrupt immediately when you hear a potential punchline
- Record immediately and get verbal confirmation ("Can I write this down?")

### Professional Standards
- Love the "role" (guest), not necessarily the artist
- Maintain confidentiality
- Do not gossip about guests publicly

### Truth Principle: Selective Truth
- Jokes should be 90% true
- Do not tell ALL the truth—only the comfortable parts
- Use true facts that are funny and safe
- Avoid harmful or unnecessary truths
