# Creative Production Techniques Glossary

## Jie-cha (接茬)

**Type**: Technique

**Definition**: Interrupting a conversation to insert a punchline or build on a comedic premise immediately.

**When to Use**:
- During pre-interviews when a guest says something with comedic potential
- In script readings when a natural comedic opportunity arises

**How to Execute**:
1. Listen actively for potential punchlines or comedic premises
2. Interrupt immediately—timing is critical
3. Build on the premise or insert the punchline
4. Record the moment and get verbal confirmation

**Example**:
- Guest: "I haven't been back to my hometown in 10 years..."
- Writer (interrupting): "What was the first thing you noticed that changed?"

## Selective Truth

**Type**: Principle

**Definition**: Using true facts that are funny and safe, while avoiding harmful or unnecessary truths.

**Core Ratio**: Jokes should be 90% true

**Application Guidelines**:
- Choose truths that are comfortable for the subject
- Avoid truths that could harm reputation or relationships
- Focus on relatable, humorous facts
- Never fabricate, but curate

**Examples**:
- ✓ Acceptable: "He's been to the same restaurant 50 times" (true, relatable, funny)
- ✗ Avoid: "He had a public meltdown at that restaurant" (true, but harmful)

## "Yes, And..." Feedback Method

**Origin**: Improvisational theater

**Application in Creative Meetings**:
1. Acknowledge the contribution ("Yes")
2. Build upon it ("And...")
3. Never block or reject without offering alternative

**Example**:
- ❌ Wrong: "That joke doesn't work."
- ✓ Right: "Yes, and what if we add a callback to the earlier scene?"

## Creation is Dictatorship Principle

**Core Concept**: While brainstorming is collaborative, final creative decisions must have a clear owner.

**Implementation**:
- Define the decision-maker before meetings begin
- Allow open brainstorming
- The responsible party makes the final call
- Team supports the decision once made

**Decision Rights by Role**:

| Content Type | Decision Maker |
|-------------|----------------|
| Guest segments | Script Editor |
| Performance delivery | Actor |
| Overall show direction | Director/Producer |
