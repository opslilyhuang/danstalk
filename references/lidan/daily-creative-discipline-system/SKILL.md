# Daily Creative Discipline System

## Daily Volume Practice

**Write immediately upon waking:**
- Write 5 jokes (段子) every day
- Revise/improve these jokes before sleeping
- Reference principle: Liu Guoliang's training - "This volume is enough."

## Commercial Work as Training

**Accept assigned writing:**
- Treat commercial/assigned work (行活儿) as practice opportunities
- Apply "replacement of training with competition" (以赛代练)
- Do not look down on commercial work
- Many masters (e.g., Xin Shuang, Wu Ershan) started there

## Overcoming Writer's Block

**Never wait for inspiration:**
- Core mantra: "Creation requires striking oneself, not waiting for lightning" (创作不能靠雷劈)

**Self-prompting methods:**
- Flip open an idiom dictionary
- Pick a random idiom, read the definition
- Write from that theme (Liang Zuo's method)

**Use external pressure:**
- Set deadlines to force action
- Use past show themes (e.g., from 'Roast Con') to practice

## Action First Mentality

**Embrace imperfection:**
- "Done is better than perfect" (完成比完美重要)
- Write badly first to get started
- Bad drafts lead to good ones
- Accept "Eyes high, hands low" (high taste, low skill) as reality

## Quantity Over Quality

**Volume is the core secret:**
- Neil Gaiman principle: Write 300,000 words of garbage to prepare for 200,000 words of good work
- Avoid the "Critic" mindset which destroys creativity

**Muscle growth analogy:**
- Muscles grow by tearing (over-exertion)
- The brain is the same
- "Over-creation" (painful repetition) is necessary for evolution
- When you feel "unwilling to write anymore," that is the breakthrough point

## Inspiration Principle

**Inspiration visits workers:**
- Inspiration visits those who work, not those who wait
- Daily writing creates the conditions for inspiration to strike
- Establish rhythm - inspiration follows established patterns
