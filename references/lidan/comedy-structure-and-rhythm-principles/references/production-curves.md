## Production Curves: Show vs. Variety Show

### Show (The Drop Curve)
- Text/Highest point: Looks best on paper
- Performance: ~80% effect lost (especially with guests)
- Broadcast: "Screen decay" (效果打折扣), lowest point

### Variety Show (The Rise Curve)
- Recording/Low point: Often chaotic or unsure at start
- Process: Massive shooting, pressure designs, extreme competition
- Broadcast/High point: Accidental endings, human nature revealed, post-production editing creates peak

### Strategy
- Combine Show ability (strong text for high starting point)
- With Variety mindset (strong design for high process points)
